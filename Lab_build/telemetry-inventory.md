# Overview of telemetry inventory

This doc will show the telemetry mappings and schemas used throughout this overall engagement.
Showing limitations and drawbacks of the chosen methods, and their efficiency at covering ground. 
The purpose of this testing engagement is to discover if the logging works on all of the 5 VMs, before trying to move it to the SIEM VM. Removing downstream time wasted troubleshooting whether or not the transmission is the issue or the telemetry itself.
